# Lock chamber visual study

Generated by `tools/havora/study_lock_chamber.py` from the current native gym metatiles and palettes. This is an isolated art proposal, not a ROM screenshot or implemented map.

The basin footprint stays fixed. A shallow rear retaining face exposes 16, 8, or 0 pixels at low/middle/high water. Three separate side landings carry clear deck lips and stone front faces; the bottom two flood in sequence. Short ladder flights are mounted on solid stone shafts. The boat rises a small amount instead of crossing the basin as the water changes.

The PNG compares settled states; the GIF illustrates the proposed transition with staged flooding. The GIF does not reproduce the game's water texture animation. Gameplay docking coordinates, walkable connections, and collision for this geometry still need implementation and validation after the visual direction is reviewed. No live gym layouts or scripts were changed by this study.
